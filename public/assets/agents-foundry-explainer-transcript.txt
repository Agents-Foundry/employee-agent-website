THE ORGANIZATIONAL LAYER FOR AI
Meet Agents Foundry, the organizational layer for AI. Give AI employees a role, a team, and a clear boundary, with people in charge.

GUIDED ONBOARDING
The guided onboarding target is five to ten minutes, once your organization and employee accounts are configured. Here is the QA assignment flow.

01 / CHOOSE A ROLE
Start in your organization's admin workspace. Choose the QA Engineer blueprint and the active employees who will receive an agent.

02 / DEFINE THE BOUNDARY
Name the agent and complete the blueprint questionnaire. Define its project scope, working environment, and configuration preferences. Review the enforced capabilities before assigning.

03 / CREATE AND ASSIGN
Create and assign. Up to twenty-five active employees can receive distinct agent instances from one setup. Each gets an employee-bound, signed assignment and separate conversation history.

04 / VERIFY AND USE
Employees open Your assigned agents and verify the agent before selecting it. The workspace checks the signature and ownership, connecting the role to the right person and organization.

05 / KEEP HUMAN CONTROL
The QA employee supports planning. Browser execution requires human approval, and production deployment is denied. An approved run is ready for the next step; connected execution remains on the roadmap.
